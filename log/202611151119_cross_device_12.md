# Cross-device table rebuilt for 12 devices

## Goal

Replace the cross-device tables (docs/benchmarks.md, README, README-ios) with
figures measured under the conditions in docs/benchmarks.md, now that every
device has been measured again: `viewer: none`, three runs, clean Release build,
median of run medians with min–max, warmup as the median across runs.

## Sources

The engine and example code is identical in every build used (`c218ebb` to
`0152ee3`; later commits change only scripts, docs, and Linux CMake).

the local summary, and the docs and README tables were generated from them
(84 rows, 12 devices × 7 benchmarks), not typed by hand.

## Decisions

- **Iris Xe published with its ranges and a footnote.** Three sessions (charging;
  full charge with rests; every other app quit) all left every row but
  pbr_envmap spreading 14–105% between runs. Temperature was logged and does not
  follow the spread. The Intel driver UI here (Intel Graphics Software 1.100,
  driver 32.0.101.7085) has no power setting, and no frequency log was possible.
  As agreed beforehand, no further sessions.
- **RTX 3090 used as measured** (run 2 15–19% faster on three rows, all runs at
  P0), with a footnote.
- **RX 9070 XT and UHD 730 added.** The README table gains an RX 9070 XT
  column, so it covers an AMD GPU.
- The table now carries the min–max column, and rows wider than 10% carry a
  numbered note: Iris Xe (¹), RTX 3090 (²), Mali-G715 heat drift (³), and the
  short image_fitting row on the phones and UHD 730 (⁴). A14 image_fitting (⁵)
  and the A14 envmap warmup clamp (⁶) keep their notes.
- The old footnotes on `live` pbr_shading, 60-frame desktop runs, and the
  `--fg-only=0` envmap rows are gone with the rows they described.
- "Current status" now lists what still falls short of the rules: the A14
  install pass, no GPU-state record on the RTX PRO 6000 / Ada main runs (a later
  sampler rerun on the PRO 6000 was P0 throughout and within 2%), no
  `nvidia-smi` on the AMD / Intel GPUs, and idle apps left open per machine.

## What moved against the old table

The old table mixed `live`, `idle`, and `none`, and one to three runs. Notable
changes, none of them a code change:

- pbr_shading with no viewer: M5 Pro 583 → 1360 FPS (now the fastest device),
  RTX PRO 6000 677 → 840, A14 86 → 75.
- Adreno 740 texture 48.4 → 32.4 ms and aa 21.6 → 16.3 ms (the old rows were
  `live`); image_fitting 0.225 → 0.386 ms (the old row was a single run, most
  likely `none`).
- Iris Xe pbr_envmap 105 → 138 ms (old row: `--fg-only=0`, one run).
- RTX 3090 (old rows: one run): hardsoftras 6.82 → 3.59 ms, aa 2.81 → 2.00,
  image_fitting 0.116 → 0.086; pbr_envmap unchanged (14.0 → 14.2).

## Observations added

- Packing follows the driver, not the vendor: the UHD 730 under Mesa fuses like
  the discrete cards (pbr_material →74) while the Iris Xe under Intel's Windows
  driver fuses least (→146).
- No phone wins every row; the A14 leads only pbr_shading.
- The RX 9070 XT sits between the RTX 3090 and the two workstation NVIDIA cards.
